# nn_r3 material gates on Sekirei v0.3.68

These two local gates compared the exact `nn_r3` HalfKP candidate with the
built-in material evaluator using the same Sekirei v0.3.68 binary. They are
candidate-qualification evidence, not an external rating or a decision to
bundle or select the network by default.

## Frozen identity

- Engine tag and commit: `v0.3.68`,
  `6b00f0eaea9b3bc1153c25f4b6ddcd33a0cfdbc7`
- Engine SHA-256:
  `705b0e5b285dbdbaa5db7489cb18238251736ec17aa165756414d01d43e7cc77`
- Candidate SHA-256:
  `075ff76cc023abd7bae07ee5cd0c2ccf6e6b4ec80942d1bfb86a2bb83ee78627`
- Opening corpus SHA-256:
  `a1bbe55e6969937e80708cd2dadbf52490370713a9f8c937fdfca9ad510f291b`
- Host: Intel Xeon 2.80 GHz, 2 vCPU, 7 GB
- Common settings: one thread, `SpecTopN=0`, `UseBook=false`, Hash 64,
  candidate `FV_SCALE=24`
- Decision rule: paired trinomial SPRT, H0=0 Elo, H1=+20 Elo,
  alpha=beta=0.05, complete colour-swapped pairs only

The openings were generated by Sekirei v0.3.68 material self-play, filtered by
the recorded `mat1000` rule, and checked for zero overlap with the named NNUE
training boards and earlier `openings_mat1000` set. The Q20 hold-out was not
opened.

## Results

| Time per move | Pairs | Score | LLR | Upper bound | Verdict |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

The 500 ms gate used openings from the start of the frozen corpus. The 1 second
gate started at opening 201, outside the earlier decision set. Engines were
restarted for every pair, and load checks recorded that only candidate-side
processes acknowledged `EvalFile`.

[`gate-500ms.json`](gate-500ms.json) and
[`gate-1000ms.json`](gate-1000ms.json) are normalized copies of the terminal
summaries. The complete local archives remain outside Git and are identified by
SHA-256 in the candidate card. Their absence, the missing per-shard training
hashes and whole-game hold-out manifest, and the lack of a current-main gate
continue to block distribution and default adoption.
